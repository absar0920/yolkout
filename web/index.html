        About Yolkout

        Yolkout is a free, fast multiplayer shooter where every player is a well-armed egg. Pick a weapon, pick a hat, and go crack some shells.

        • Free, all of it. Every weapon, hat, colour and stamp is unlocked. There are no ads, no purchases and no analytics.
        • Your data stays with you. Your name and settings live in your own browser's storage. The server only sees your display name to show it to other players and keep the leaderboard.
        • Built light. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in the browser, a single Go server, no build step. Sounds are synthesized live; models and textures are generated in code.
